Before you score

  • Tap a team's big panel to give them the point. Then pick Kill, Ace, Block or Error, and the player, if you want it on the video. Skip it and the bar closes by itself.
  • Kill / Ace / Block take turns. If you enter several quickly, each one plays for about 5 seconds, in order. A pill at the top ("2 PLAYS WAITING") shows how many are left; tap it to skip them.
  • T/O uses one of the team's timeouts for this set (the button shows how many are used). It puts a 30-second timeout banner on the video and offers the full timeout screen.
  • Serve moves the serve. Points move it automatically.
  • More has break screens, on-screen messages, hide scoreboard, the camera-phone QR code and the score log.
↶ Undo steps back the last change of any kind, one tap at a time: a point, a −1, a serve change, a timeout or a Match setup save. It is not just for points. Tap it slowly and check the score after each tap.
−1 takes one point away from that team in the current set and leaves everything after it alone. Use it to fix a mistake from a while back. It can't reach into a finished set; use Undo for that.
A Kill/Ace/Block already on the video can't be pulled back. Ones still waiting are dropped if you undo or −1 their point.
Only one phone should score a court. If two do, the last one to save wins and the other sees "Score updated from another scorer's phone".
