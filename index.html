Robins Scoreboard

A live AVA scoreboard for match streams from an iPhone with Tubeist. One phone films, another phone keeps score, and the score shows up on the stream.

  1. Camera phone: in Tubeist, go to Settings → Overlays, add the overlay link for your court, then Save.
  2. Scorer phone: open the scorer link the club sent you (it has the scorer code in it).

Another base setting up its own courts? See the team kit.
